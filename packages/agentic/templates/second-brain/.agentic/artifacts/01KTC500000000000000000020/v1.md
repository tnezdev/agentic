# Inbox Captures: Reading Queue Friction

These captures are intentionally messy. The seed task should turn them into a focused research brief, not preserve them as-is.

## Captures

- I keep saving essays about personal knowledge management, but the queue does not tell me what is worth reading next.
- I want a lightweight way to decide whether a saved item should become a task, a note, a reference, or an archive item.
- Summaries are not enough. The useful output is a recommendation: what should I do with this reading queue now?
- Weekly review should prune the queue instead of turning every interesting link into an obligation.

## Working Question

How should a small second-brain workspace structure a reading queue so saved items become useful decisions instead of a stagnant backlog?

## Decision This Supports

Choose the minimal statuses and review behavior for a public-safe reading queue example.

## Constraints

- Keep the example generic and reusable.
- Do not assume a specific notes app, browser extension, or external service.
- Prefer a workflow an agent can act on through durable Agentic primitives: tasks, workflows, artifacts, and follow-up tasks.

## Useful Next Move
